# Product specification

MyQuiz is a MEC CEE preparation system, not a generic quiz builder. Student scoring and eligibility are server-authoritative. Only questions that are both verified and published are eligible for scored practice. AI extraction may stage content but can never establish an uncertain answer key.

The initial supported authoritative blueprint is Group I (MBBS, BDS, BSc Nursing/BSc Midwifery, BASLP and B. Perfusion Technology). Groups II–IV remain represented in the source model and must be seeded only from their exact syllabus tables.

Core product areas are authentication, verified question bank, reusable quiz engine, attempts/review, analytics, deterministic recommendations, adaptive practice, FSRS flashcards, community, progression, entitlements, contributions and private storage, durable extraction, CSV, AI assistance, authorized external ingestion, and staff administration.
